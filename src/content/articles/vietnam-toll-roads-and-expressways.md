## The Modern Expressway Network in Northern Vietnam

Over the past decade, Vietnam has invested heavily in modern limited-access expressway corridors (designated with the prefix **CT**). These expressways connect Hanoi directly to premier travel destinations:

- **CT01 (North-South Expressway):** Connects Hanoi to Ninh Binh (Phap Van – Cau Gie – Cao Bo – Mai Son) in approximately 1.5 hours.
- **CT04 & CT06 (Hanoi – Hai Phong – Ha Long):** Modern 6-lane highway connecting Hanoi to Halong Bay cruise ports in roughly 2.0 to 2.5 hours via the Bach Dang Cable-Stayed Bridge.
- **CT05 (Noi Bai – Lao Cai):** The 245 km spine road to Sapa and northern border provinces, bypassing congested local towns.

## Why Private Vehicles Use Expressways

- **Strict Motorbike Prohibition:** Unlike traditional National Highways (such as QL1A or QL18), motorbikes, bicycles, and pedestrians are strictly prohibited from entering expressways. This dramatically reduces collision risks and allows steady cruising speeds of 100 to 120 km/h.
- **Bypassing Congested Towns:** Expressways route traffic entirely outside provincial markets and suburban traffic signals, cutting journey times by 40% to 50% compared to legacy routes.

## Electronic Toll Collection (ETC)

All expressway toll plazas in Vietnam operate exclusively via 100% automated **Electronic Toll Collection (ETC)**:

- Every licensed vehicle in our fleet is equipped with a certified RFID e-tag (VETC or ePass) mounted to the windscreen.
- Tolls are deducted automatically without requiring the vehicle to stop at toll barrier booths.
- **All-Inclusive Flat Pricing:** In our rental quotes, all electronic expressway toll charges are fully calculated and included in advance, meaning you will never be asked to pay cash toll surcharges during your trip.
