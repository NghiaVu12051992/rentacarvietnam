Choosing the right private vehicle in Vietnam depends on two essential factors: **the number of passengers and the total volume of luggage.** 

Unlike local taxis or public transit, private charters require adequate dedicated luggage boot or cargo space so that every passenger rides comfortably without bags occupying seating rows or aisles. Here is our practical fleet sizing guide to help you select the ideal vehicle for your itinerary.

---

## 7-Seater Car (SUV / MPV)

- **Capacity with Luggage:** Best for **1–3 passengers with luggage**.
- **Maximum Capacity (No Luggage):** Up to 4–5 passengers for short city transfers or dining excursions.
- **Luggage Capacity:** 2–3 standard suitcases (24–28 inch) plus carry-ons in the rear boot.
- **Ideal For:** Couples, solo business travelers, small families, and airport transfers between Noi Bai Airport and central Hanoi hotels.
- **Popular Models:** Toyota Innova, Toyota Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander.
- **Explore Vehicle:** [View 7-Seater Car Specifications](/vehicles/7-seater-car/)

---

## 16-Seater Passenger Van (Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati)

- **Capacity with Luggage:** Best for **4–9 passengers with luggage**.
- **Maximum Capacity (No Luggage):** Up to a **maximum of 15 passengers without luggage** (for Hanoi city tours or event shuttles).
- **Luggage Capacity:** With 4–9 travelers, the rear seating row easily folds forward to accommodate 6–9 full-size suitcases plus backpacks.
- **Ideal For:** Medium families, friend groups, golf excursions, and overland transfers to Halong Bay, Ninh Binh, or Sapa.
- **Popular Models:** Ford Transit Luxury, Hyundai Solati.
- **VIP Upgrade Option:** For travelers seeking luxury captain chairs, our [9-Seater VIP Limousine](/vehicles/9-seater-limousine/) also comfortably accommodates 5–7 passengers with luggage.

## 29-Seater Midibus (Hyundai County / Thaco Town)

- **Capacity with Luggage:** Best for **up to 16 passengers with luggage** (10–16 passengers).
- **Maximum Capacity (No Luggage):** Up to a **maximum of 28 passengers without luggage**.
- **Luggage Capacity:** Dedicated rear cargo compartment and underfloor bays holding 14–16 large suitcases.
- **Ideal For:** Corporate groups, school and university field trips, multi-family vacations, and regional golf tours.
- **Popular Models:** Hyundai County, Thaco Garden, Isuzu Samco.
- **Explore Vehicle:** [View 29-Seater Bus Specifications](/vehicles/29-seater-bus/)

## 35-Seater Bus (Thaco Meadow / Samco Felix)

- **Capacity with Luggage:** Best for **up to 24 passengers with luggage** (17–24 passengers).
- **Maximum Capacity (No Luggage):** Up to a **maximum of 34 passengers without luggage**.
- **Luggage Capacity:** Spacious underfloor luggage compartments designed to hold 20–24 check-in suitcases.
- **Ideal For:** Company incentive retreats, conference delegates, international tourist groups, and medium-sized sports teams.
- **Popular Models:** Thaco Kinglong, Hyundai Aero Town, Samco Felix.

## 45-Seater Coach (Hyundai Universe / Thaco Bluesky)

- **Capacity with Luggage:** Accommodates **up to a maximum of 45 passengers with luggage** (25–45 passengers).
- **Luggage Capacity:** High-capacity underbelly luggage bays designed to carry checked baggage and equipment cases for every passenger on board.
- **Ideal For:** Large tour groups, international MICE conferences, university delegations, and nationwide sightseeing circuits.

## Quick Sizing Summary: With Luggage vs. Without Luggage

When planning your vehicle booking, always differentiate between **airport/overland transfers** (which involve luggage) and **city day tours** (without luggage):

- **7-Seater Car:** 1–3 passengers with luggage | Up to 5 passengers without luggage
- **16-Seater Van:** 4–9 passengers with luggage | Up to 15 passengers without luggage
- **29-Seater Bus:** Up to 16 passengers with luggage | Up to 28 passengers without luggage
- **35-Seater Bus:** Up to 24 passengers with luggage | Up to 34 passengers without luggage
- **45-Seater Coach:** Up to 45 passengers with luggage (full touring capacity)

---

## Helpful Luggage Sizing Tips

- **Luggage Demands Space:** If your group travels with oversized luggage, such as golf bags, baby strollers, folding wheelchairs, or hard-shell 28-inch luggage, **always select the next vehicle tier up** even if the total head count fits a smaller model.
- **Comfort on Long Journeys:** For expressway and mountain journeys longer than 3 hours (such as Hanoi to Ha Giang or Sapa), leaving extra cabin breathing room ensures a significantly more relaxing experience.
