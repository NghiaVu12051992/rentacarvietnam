## Start with passengers and luggage

Share the number of adults and children travelling, together with the number and size of your bags. A vehicle's advertised seat count does not describe how much luggage it can carry with every seat occupied.

Mention strollers, wheelchairs, golf bags or other bulky items when requesting a quote. Ask the team to confirm the seating and luggage arrangement for the vehicle offered.

## Match the vehicle to your journey

- For an airport pickup, allow space for every suitcase as well as passengers.
- For a day with several stops, consider ease of getting in and out of the vehicle.
- For a longer journey, ask about seating comfort, luggage access and rest stops.
- For a large group, share any requirements for a tour guide or separate luggage storage.

## Confirm the model and arrangement

Review the proposed vehicle category, passenger capacity and luggage allowance in your written quote. If you need a particular model or a child seat, ask for confirmation before booking.
