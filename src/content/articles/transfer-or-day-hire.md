## A transfer takes you between locations

A private transfer covers an agreed pickup and drop-off. It suits a journey from your hotel to another city, an airport or a cruise port. Confirm the addresses, departure time and any planned stops in advance.

## Day hire keeps the vehicle with your group

Full-day hire is useful when you want to visit several places and have the driver wait between stops. Share the full itinerary so the quote can specify the service hours, distance allowance and waiting arrangements.

## A return transfer is a separate choice

A return journey does not automatically include a vehicle waiting throughout the day or local sightseeing. Specify whether you need two transfers or a driver to stay with you.

## What to check in your quote

- Pickup and final drop-off addresses.
- Service dates and approximate finishing time.
- Stops, waiting time and local travel at the destination.
- Included hours and kilometres, plus any additional charges.
