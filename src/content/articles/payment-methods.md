## Overview of Payment Options

To make securing your private chauffeur service as convenient and reliable as possible, we offer three primary international payment methods. Whether you prefer direct corporate wire, fast online credit card checkout, or global remittance, our team ensures complete transaction transparency and data security.

---

## 1. Company Bank Transfer (USD)

You can transfer funds directly to our company's corporate USD bank account in Vietnam via international wire transfer (SWIFT / Telegraphic Transfer - TT).

- **Best Suited For:** Multi-day private tours, overland journeys, luxury limousine bookings, coach/bus charters, and corporate business accounts.
- **How It Works:** Once your itinerary and vehicle are agreed upon, our finance team will issue a formal proforma invoice containing our corporate bank details:
  - **Account Name:** Corporate Legal Entity
  - **USD Account Number:** Provided on invoice
  - **SWIFT / BIC Code:** Bank identifier code
  - **Beneficiary Bank:** Joint Stock Commercial Bank in Vietnam
- **Important Note:** Please ensure all intermediary and sender-side bank transfer fees are selected as "OUR" so the exact invoice deposit reaches our account.

---

## 2. OnePay Online Payment Gateway – Visa Card

Pay securely, instantly, and conveniently online using your Visa card (as well as MasterCard and JCB) through OnePay, Vietnam's premier licensed payment processing gateway.

- **Instant & Seamless:** Our dispatch coordinator generates a personalized, encrypted payment link sent straight to your email or WhatsApp. No app installation or account sign-up is required.
- **Bank-Grade Security:** Transactions are processed under 256-bit TLS encryption, full PCI-DSS compliance, and mandatory **3D-Secure** verification (one-time OTP code sent by your bank).
- **Dispute & Refund Protection:** If a service delivery issue or payment dispute arises, you may be eligible to request a refund through the applicable dispute process within **30 days**, governed by OnePay's terms and the relevant card issuer's or bank's international dispute resolution policies.

---

## 3. Western Union

For travelers who prefer cash or over-the-counter international money transfers, we accept payments via Western Union online, through the mobile app, or at any global agent location.

- **Recipient Credentials:** Upon booking agreement, our operations manager will supply the official recipient details (full legal name, identity number, and city).
- **Fast Clearance:** After completing your transfer, simply send the 10-digit **Money Transfer Control Number (MTCN)** and the sender's full legal name to our dispatch team via WhatsApp or email. We verify the payment and confirm your vehicle immediately.

---

## Deposit Requirements & Payment Schedule

To reserve your designated vehicle and assign a professional chauffeur, payment follows a simple two-tier policy:

- **Bookings under US$ 20:** Full payment (100%) is required upon confirmation.
- **Bookings of US$ 20 or more:** A **30% deposit** is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance can be settled prior to departure or directly as agreed in your written confirmation.

---

## Currency & Exchange Rates

- Invoices and quotations are provided in **US Dollars (USD)** or **Vietnam Dong (VND)**.
- If paying via OnePay or domestic transfer in VND, transactions are converted using the official Vietcombank foreign exchange rate on the day of payment.
- Official corporate electronic VAT invoices (e-VAT) are available upon request for corporate clients and business travelers.
