## Overview of Noi Bai International Airport (HAN)

Noi Bai Airport is located approximately 28 km (17 miles) north of central Hanoi. The airport has two separate passenger terminals located roughly 1 km apart:

- **Terminal 2 (T2):** Dedicated to all international flights arriving from overseas.
- **Terminal 1 (T1):** Dedicated to all domestic flights within Vietnam (such as flights from Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City, or Nha Trang).

## Meeting Your Chauffeur at International Terminal 2 (T2)

After landing, clearing immigration passport control, collecting your baggage, and passing customs inspection, you will enter the main ground-floor arrivals hall:

- Walk through the automatic sliding glass doors toward the exterior curbside walkway.
- Your dedicated chauffeur will be waiting in the arrival greeting area, typically positioned between **Column 9 and Column 10**, holding a clear signboard with your lead passenger name.
- If you plan to purchase a local tourist SIM card or exchange currency inside the terminal, please let dispatch know on WhatsApp so your driver remains ready.

## Meeting Your Chauffeur at Domestic Terminal 1 (T1)

Terminal 1 handles domestic flight arrivals:

- Domestic baggage claim leads directly to the ground floor arrival concourses (Hall A, B, or E depending on your airline).
- Your chauffeur will coordinate via WhatsApp and greet you at the outer designated passenger pickup curb adjacent to your arrival exit door.
