## Your essential trip details

- Travel date and preferred pickup time.
- Pickup address and destination.
- Number of adults and children.
- Number and size of suitcases.
- One-way travel, return travel or hire with the driver staying with you.

## Details for airport or cruise connections

Include your flight number or cruise name, the relevant terminal or port, and any boarding deadline. Explain whether the time you provide is your arrival time or your requested vehicle pickup time.

## Special requests

Mention accessibility needs, child seats, extra luggage and any stops you want to make. For a multi-day trip, share the planned overnight locations and final drop-off point.

## Review before confirming

Check the vehicle, itinerary, inclusions and payment and cancellation terms in the written offer. Ask the team to clarify anything that does not match your request.
