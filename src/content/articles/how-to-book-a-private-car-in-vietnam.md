## Step 1: Send Us Your Travel Request

Start by sharing your travel schedule and passenger details through our website quote wizard, email, or direct WhatsApp message. To calculate an accurate and transparent net quote, please include:

- **Travel Date & Preferred Pickup Time:** Exact calendar date and departure time (e.g., 08:00 AM hotel pickup or flight arrival time).
- **Pickup & Drop-off Locations:** Specific addresses, hotel names, or airport terminal details (e.g., Hanoi Old Quarter to Halong Bay, Da Nang Airport to Hoi An).
- **Number of Passengers & Luggage Count:** Total number of adults, children, and number of standard checked suitcases (24–28 inch) and carry-on bags.
- **Planned Route & Custom Stops:** Any sightseeing stops, photography viewpoints, meal breaks, or intermediate transfers along the highway.

---

## Step 2: Receive Your Personalized Route & Fleet Recommendation

Within **2 to 12 hours** of receiving your inquiry, our operations coordinator will review your schedule and respond with:

- **Tailored Vehicle Recommendation:** Selecting the right vehicle class for your group size and luggage volume (4-seater sedan, 7-seater SUV, 16-seater passenger van, or 9/11-seater luxury VIP limousine).
- **Actual Fleet Photos & Specs:** Transparent photos showing recent models (2024–2026 fleet), seating configurations, and boot capacity so you know exactly what vehicle will arrive.
- **Transparent All-Inclusive Net Quote:** Our quotes include the professional chauffeur, clean air-conditioned vehicle, fuel, highway tolls (expressway fees), parking charges, and airport flight monitoring. There are no hidden surcharges.

---

## Step 3: Confirm Your Booking & Place Deposit

Once you are satisfied with the proposed vehicle type, schedule, and pricing, we will issue a formal booking confirmation invoice. Payment terms follow our clear two-tier structure:

- **Bookings under US$ 20:** Full payment is required upon confirmation to finalize dispatch.
- **Bookings of US$ 20 or more:** A **30% deposit** is required to secure the vehicle and driver on our calendar. The remaining balance can be settled prior to departure or directly as agreed in your written confirmation.

You can settle your payment securely via **Company Bank Transfer (USD)**, **OnePay Online Gateway (Visa Card)**, or **Western Union**.

---

## Step 4: Passenger Manifest & Pickup Coordination

Under Vietnamese transportation regulations and to ensure seamless greeting at airports or hotels, please share the following details after deposit confirmation:

1. **Passenger Information (Primary Contact & Group Manifest):**
   - Full name as printed in passport for each traveler
   - Passport number & passport expiry date
   - Date of birth & nationality
2. **Airport Arrivals (if applicable):**
   - Flight number & operating airline
   - Scheduled landing time
   - Terminal (International T2 or Domestic T1)
3. **Hotel / Villa Pickups:**
   - Full hotel name and address
   - Room number (can be sent via WhatsApp on the morning of pickup)

### Why We Collect Passport & Flight Details:
- **Flight Delay Tracking:** If your inbound flight is delayed or rescheduled, our dispatch team tracks the radar in real time and automatically reschedules your driver without missing your arrival.
- **Identification & Safety:** Your driver will hold a personalized welcome signboard with your primary name at the arrivals exit gate, avoiding confusion with unofficial airport touts.

---

## Step 5: Day of Service & Meeting Your Driver

- **Driver Contact Shared in Advance:** 12 to 24 hours before your trip, our customer support desk will send your assigned driver’s name, contact number, vehicle model, and registration plate via WhatsApp or email.
- **Airport Meet & Greet:** For airport transfers, your driver waits outside the baggage claim arrival hall holding a clear name sign. Driver waiting time is complimentary up to 60 minutes after flight touchdown.
- **Hotel Pickups:** Your driver arrives 10–15 minutes early in the hotel lobby or outside your villa driveway.

---

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Please review our policy carefully before confirming your booking:

- **Non-Refundable Policy:** **All payments and deposits are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.** Because we reserve dedicated vehicles and assign professional drivers exclusively to your itinerary, canceling means those slots cannot be reallocated on short notice.
- **Flexible Rescheduling (1 Time):** If your travel schedule changes due to airline adjustments or personal reasons, you may reschedule your departure date **once**, subject to:
  1. Fleet and driver availability for the new requested date.
  2. A rescheduling administration fee of **10% of the total booking value**.
  3. Notice must be sent to our team at least **24 hours prior** to the original pickup time.

## Helpful Tips for a Smooth Trip

1. **Luggage Planning:** If your group has oversized baggage (golf bags, baby strollers, surfboards), always choose the next vehicle class up (e.g., book a 16-seater van for 6 passengers with lots of luggage instead of a 7-seater SUV).
2. **Keep WhatsApp Active:** We recommend getting an eSIM or local Vietnam SIM card upon landing so our operations desk and driver can coordinate with you instantly.
