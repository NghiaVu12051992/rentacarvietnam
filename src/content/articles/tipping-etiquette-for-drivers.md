## Is Tipping Compulsory in Vietnam?

Tipping is **not mandatory or customary** in everyday Vietnamese culture (such as at local street food stalls or market shops). However, in international tourism and private chauffeur services, tipping has become a widely appreciated gesture to reward safe, patient, and courteous service.

Our chauffeurs receive professional salaries, travel allowances, and fuel provisions. Tipping is entirely at your personal discretion based on your overall satisfaction with the journey.

## Typical Tipping Guidelines

If you feel your driver delivered attentive care—such as safely navigating tight mountain passes, maintaining pristine vehicle cleanliness, and patiently waiting during excursions—the following ranges represent customary gratuity benchmarks:

- **Airport Transfers:** 50,000 to 100,000 VND (~$2 to $4 USD) per group.
- **Full-Day City Tour or Day Trip (Ninh Binh, Halong):** 150,000 to 250,000 VND (~$6 to $10 USD) per group.
- **Multi-Day Mountain Expeditions (Sapa, Ha Giang, Cao Bang):** 200,000 to 350,000 VND (~$8 to $15 USD) per day for the group.

## Our Zero-Solicitation Policy

We enforce a strict professional standard across our team:

- Chauffeurs will never solicit, hint, or demand tips from passengers.
- All quoted vehicle rates are comprehensive, with fuel, tolls, and driver allowances fully settled in your booking agreement.
- Any tip you choose to give should be handed directly to your chauffeur in cash (VND or USD) at the conclusion of your service.
