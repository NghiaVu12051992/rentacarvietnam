## Requesting a change

Contact the dispatch team with your booking reference, travel date and requested changes. Ask for written confirmation of any revised itinerary, availability or price.

## Cancellation terms

Request the cancellation deadline, any charges and any refund arrangements before confirming your booking. Refer to the terms supplied with your written offer.

## Delays and missed connections

If your flight or plans change, contact the team with the updated details and ask how the change affects your pickup arrangements.
