## Cancellation & Non-Refundable Policy

When you book a private vehicle with our service, we allocate a dedicated modern vehicle and reserve a professional chauffeur exclusively for your itinerary. Because these resources are committed in advance and cannot be reallocated on short notice:

- **All payments and deposits are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.**
- We do not offer cash or card refunds for client-initiated cancellations, change of travel mind, or missed departures.
- We strongly recommend that all international travelers acquire comprehensive travel insurance to cover unexpected cancellations, personal medical emergencies, or flight disruptions.

---

## One-Time Rescheduling Policy

We understand that travel itineraries can change. If you need to adjust your departure date, you may reschedule your journey **once**, subject to the following clear conditions:

1. **Advance Notice:** You must notify our dispatch team in writing (via WhatsApp or email) at least **24 hours prior** to your scheduled pickup time.
2. **Vehicle & Driver Availability:** The revised date is subject to vehicle class availability on our fleet calendar.
3. **Rescheduling Fee:** A modest administration and fleet reallocation fee of **10% of the total booking value** will apply to process the adjustment.

---

## Inbound Flight Delays & Missed Connections

If your flight into Vietnam is delayed or rescheduled by the airline, **you will not be charged a cancellation fee**, provided you have supplied your flight number in advance:

- Our airport dispatch team tracks international and domestic flights in real time via live radar.
- Your driver will automatically adjust the pickup time according to your flight's actual touchdown time.
- Complimentary driver waiting time is provided for up to **60 minutes** after flight touchdown to allow you to clear immigration and collect baggage.
- If your flight is diverted or cancelled outright by the airline, please notify us immediately so we can coordinate the next available vehicle for your rescheduled arrival.
