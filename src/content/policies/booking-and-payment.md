## Booking confirmation

Submitting a quote request is an inquiry. Ask the dispatch team for written confirmation of the vehicle, itinerary and agreed price before treating the journey as booked.

## Payment arrangements

Request the applicable payment method, currency, deposit requirements and payment schedule with your quotation.

## Questions about your booking

Use the [contact page](/contact/) and include your travel date and any booking reference when asking the team to clarify your arrangements.
