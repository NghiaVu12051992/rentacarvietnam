## Booking Confirmation & Deposit Structure

Submitting a quote request on our website initiates an initial inquiry. A booking is formally confirmed and locked onto our fleet dispatch schedule only after you have agreed upon the vehicle class, itinerary, schedule, and net quote, and settled the deposit according to our standard policy:

- **Bookings under US$ 20:** Full payment (100%) is required upon confirmation to finalize dispatch.
- **Bookings of US$ 20 or more:** A **30% deposit** is required to secure the vehicle and driver on our calendar. The remaining balance can be settled prior to departure or directly as agreed in your written booking confirmation.

## Accepted Payment Methods

We offer three secure and convenient payment methods for domestic and international travelers:

### 1. Company Bank Transfer (USD)
You can transfer funds directly to our company's corporate USD bank account in Vietnam via international wire (SWIFT / Telegraphic Transfer - TT).

- **Recommended for:** Multi-day tours, interprovincial transfers, luxury limousine bookings, coach/bus charters, and corporate accounts.
- **Bank details:** Our team will provide our official banking credentials (beneficiary account name, USD account number, SWIFT/BIC code, and beneficiary bank address) alongside your proforma invoice upon booking confirmation.
- **Bank fees:** Senders are responsible for intermediary and sending bank charges (OUR option) to ensure the full invoice deposit arrives in our account.

### 2. OnePay Online Payment Gateway – Visa Card
Pay securely and instantly online using your Visa card (as well as MasterCard and JCB) through OnePay, Vietnam’s leading licensed and 3D-Secure certified payment gateway.

- **Instant process:** Our dispatch desk generates a unique, encrypted payment link sent directly to your email or WhatsApp. No account registration or mobile app installation is needed.
- **Top-tier security:** All transactions are protected by 256-bit TLS encryption, PCI-DSS compliance, and mandatory 3D-Secure authentication (one-time OTP verification).
- **Dispute & Refund terms:** If a payment dispute arises regarding service delivery, you may be eligible to request a refund through the applicable dispute process within 30 days, subject to OnePay’s terms and the relevant card issuer's or bank's international dispute resolution policies.

### 3. Western Union
International travelers who prefer cash or over-the-counter international money transfers can pay via Western Union online, through the mobile app, or at any global Western Union agent office.

- **Recipient credentials:** Our coordinator will provide the exact recipient name, national ID/passport information, and destination city once your itinerary is agreed upon.
- **Fast clearance:** Once the transfer is completed, simply share the 10-digit Money Transfer Control Number (MTCN) and the sender’s full legal name with our team on WhatsApp or email for immediate verification.

## Currency & Invoicing

- Quotations and invoices are quoted in **US Dollars (USD)** or **Vietnam Dong (VND)**.
- If paying via OnePay or domestic transfer in VND, transactions are converted using the official Vietcombank foreign exchange rate on the date of transaction.
- Official corporate electronic VAT invoices (e-VAT) are available upon request for corporate clients and business travelers.
